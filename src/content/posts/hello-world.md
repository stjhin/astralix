### AI in July 2026

With AI, society has slowly changed. I see more and more new job titles that didn't exist just a month ago. July 2026 seems to be the time when company test how AI scale up. I think that the problem won't be so much on execution from now on, but would be about critical thinking and being able to own judgement. AI do tend to hallucinate, and people's job now seems to be to snap them out of it. I do notice that before even your resume/cv get to a real human, AI shortlisted it into the yes pile and the instant rejection pile. I find that there's a high chance of hallucination through the front door, that I'm looking at alternative like backdoor.

### Mass Unemployment by AI

I think this is fear coming from human as a species being replaced by a more intelligent life form that they created themselves. I don't think AI will be able to fully replaced human. Not that it's not able to do so intelligent wise, but being human is more just than being intelligent. AI has no emotion. For certain tasks in certain jobs, it will never be able to reproduce that human touch. I think if it'll change is that it will split jobs into traditional jobs vs post-AI jobs. It's similar to how Graphic Designer > UX Designer > Product Designer. I think the new job title based on what startups are hiring in Silicon Valley, they will be called Product Builder. I suspect that instead of craft as an execution skill, craft will be about judgement to decide is this even the right problem to solve and about being able to influence cross-functional team based on evidence and data. The teams who are used to performing being a visual designer, will slowly disappear as leaderships realize that they're not producing any profitable outcome. I suspect something similar will happen in most execution heavy jobs.

### Nostalgia

So, totally not connected. I've been watching people vlogging their life away from civilization, where everything is basically offgrid. I quite enjoy hearing all the cricket in the background and watching them doing their daily life, which consists of no electrical tools. Kitchen stove is still using charcoal. Work is basically planting food and cleaning up to make sure that things don't invite mice and other pests. I think that I quite envy this kind of simple lifestyle where you only live off the land. The food basically has 0 preservatives and you know how it's made and created. He basically has 0 income, so no need to worry when tax season comes. No AI can ever replace him too until AI can be inhabit a robot. Though, with how much it hallucinates, it'll be quite scary. I guess it's why nostalgic videos like living offgrid interests me as they made me think of the What If? question.
