I am doing a 2-week lifestyle experiment and I am a day away from the 2-week mark. The result has provided me with the right balance of how I should handle my net carb on eating days.

---

### My experiment

I started the experiment on July 15. I limit how many times I eat and on average eat every 60-72 hours. I eat once in between and ensure that I consume around 50-70g of net carb, I also didn't prevent cookies and chocolates consumption. I have extended water fasting experience. My longest was 35-days. When I fast 7-10-14 days, I would always end up with a constant ravenous binge afterwards. I want a long-term sustainable low carb keto and water fasting lifestyle. The benefit is health and financial. I get healthy and spend less money on food. I suspect people would eventually be experimenting with eating less than the 3x daily meals due to the rise in living costs all around the world.

### During experiment

I have consumed 1 chocolate babka and sometimes with 1 chocolate rugalach, with 1 capuccino with regular milk. I eat this after I eat my usual fat+protein bomb meal. I have a theory that I won't get that previous desire of ravenous binge if I do a 'carb pulse'. I would be able to trick my body so that it keeps its thyroid and leptin levels up (preventing metabolic slowdown) while satisfying my emotional and mental cravings during each fast cycle. I was right after finishing 4 fasting cycle for almost 2-weeks. I have 0 cravings, I have no desire for ravenous binge, and everytime I entered 30th hour when my body finish its transition back to ketone as a fule, I lose any feeling or desire of food. I truly feel that I have let go of food. This is emotionally, medically, and financially doable. I eat less, but I'm eating more high quality real food. My meal consists of: 4x boiled eggs, soup with meat or canned fish, and my emotional sweets. I can get creative and fit cheese, yogurt, and nuts if my macro allowance still has extra. My goal is to sustain this life style long-term. I'm not interested in spreading this. The experiment is for me to explore the best lifestyle for my own self.
